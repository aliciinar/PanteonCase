# InfoScreen

## Purpose
The selected building or unit inside the information panel (a screen of GameplayModule, listed on GameplaySystemRoot, layer 3 over the InformationScreen's body). A building shows its image, name and health, and for a building that produces units a card per unit. Each card is built with its whole `UnitSpawnRequestVO` - the unit, and the building's door and spawn cells from `BuildingInfoVO` - so clicking it announces `Outgoing.UnitRequested(request)`, which the Connector carries to the units module. A unit (`ShowUnitInfo(UnitInfoVO)`) shows its image, name, health and damage, and no production section. The configs are read through `ISharedDataModel` - CD_Buildings is filed in the Shared Scriptables of BuildingsSystemRoot's adapter, CD_Units in UnitsSystemRoot's - so the signals carry only what changes during play (type, hp, cells).

`IInfoScreenModel.Shown` (None / Building / Unit) is the one thing kept: a press on a unit clears the building's selection and makes the unit's, in two modules and in no order the screen can rely on, so `HideBuildingInfo` / `HideUnitInfo` (both `HideInfoScreenCommand`, bound with the kind) close the screen only while it still shows that kind.
