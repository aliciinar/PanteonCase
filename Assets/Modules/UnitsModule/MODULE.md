# Units

## Purpose
Owns the units of the game: the UnitType every other module names a soldier by, and CD_Units - the one place a unit is defined and every value a designer tunes about units lives: per unit its name, sprite, health, damage per strike, walking speed, strike duration and lunge reach (the share of the way to the target the lunge goes); for all of them the selection colour, the hit flash (`HitFlashCVO`: colour, duration), the explosion a destroyed unit leaves (`UnitExplosionCVO`: sprites, size in cells, start scale, duration), and the messages a refused order shows. The model hands the two CVOs out as they are - no copy. Nothing of it is on the `BoardUnit` prefab or in code; static styling on the prefab (the health bar's sprite colours) stays there.

A unit's data is the grid's `BoardUnitVO` - this module keeps no record of it. Spawning (`Incoming.SpawnUnit`) puts one on the grid at its goal and walks it out of the building's door. The grid says what a press landed on: `Incoming.SelectUnit(BoardUnitVO)` selects and colours it and announces `UnitSelected(UnitInfoVO(type, hp))`, which the info screen shows (name, image, health, damage) - again on a re-press, so the info shows its health as it is now (the same rule as Buildings); `Incoming.ClearSelection` - a building, a free cell, off the grid, or any press over UI (Input's `PointerPressedOverUI`: the production menu, the information panel, a button) - makes it wear white again and announces `SelectionCleared` (only when a unit was selected; a destroyed selected unit announces it too). `IUnitSelectionModel` keeps the selected unit, because a later secondary press orders it: `Incoming.MoveSelectedUnit(cell)` → `PlanUnitMoveCommand` (A* from the unit's cell, around buildings; the unit takes the new cell with `IGridService.MoveOccupant`) → `BoardUnits/WalkBoardUnitCommand` (world points). `Incoming.AttackWithSelectedUnit(target)` → `PlanAttackCommand` (strike from where the unit stands when it is next to the target, otherwise from `IGridService.FindFreeCellAroundBfs(target.Area, unit.Cell)` walked to by A*) → `BoardUnits/StrikeWithBoardUnitCommand` → the unit walks up, lunges once (`CD_Units.StrikeDuration`), and the lunge landing (`UnitStruck` → `StrikeCommand`) announces `Outgoing.AttackLanded(target, Damage)` - the grid lowers the target's health. A struck unit flashes and shows a health bar (`Incoming.UnitDamaged`); a destroyed one (`Incoming.RemoveUnit`) loses its selection, a puff grows and fades where it stood, and its object goes back to the pool.

**Destroyed units explode.** `RemoveBoardUnitCommand` takes a `BoardExplosion` from the pool (group `units`, key `unit_explosion` - pool keys are global, so Buildings has its own `building_explosion`), gives it one of `CD_Units.Explosion.Sprites` at a random turn, and grows it from `StartScale` of its width to `Size` cells while it fades over `Duration` (DOTween: scale + `DOTween.ToAlpha` - `SpriteRenderer.DOFade` lives in a DOTween module the module assemblies do not see); the puff goes back to the pool when it is over, the unit's object at once. Art: four of Kenney's Smoke Particles explosions (CC0) downscaled to 64 px, `Art/Explosion/Explosion1-4.png`, in the Board atlas; sorting order 8, over the health bars.

**A refused order is told to the player.** An attack with no free cell next to its target, or an attack or move with no way there, stops its flow and announces `Outgoing.OrderRefused(string)` with CD_Units' `NoRoomToAttackMessage` / `NoWayMessage`; UnitsConnectorSubContext carries it to the popup screen's `ShowPopup`. The unit stays where it is.

**The game is played one action at a time.** Spawning, moving and attacking each end their sequence with `Outgoing.ActionStarted`; the `BoardUnit` sequence finishing (a frame later at the earliest, even with nothing to play) announces `Outgoing.ActionEnded`. The gameplay module locks `RD_GameStatus` in between; `IUnitsModel.IsGameLocked` stops a spawn asked while locked.

**No view maps data to objects.** A unit's `BoardUnitVO.View` is its pooled `BoardUnit` (under UnitsSystemRoot), so a cell leads to the unit and the unit to its object. `BoardUnit` only holds references and its running tweens; the work on it - placing it, walking, striking, colouring, showing a hit, returning it to the pool - is done by the commands and functions in `Controllers/BoardUnits/`, whose tweens call back through the internal `UnitStruck` / `UnitActionFinished`.

## Known gaps
- Units walk through one another while walking; each still stops on a cell of its own.
- A strike reaches one cell: a target with every cell next to it taken cannot be attacked (the player is told so).
- A spawn with no free cell on the board makes no unit and tells the player nothing (only a log line).
