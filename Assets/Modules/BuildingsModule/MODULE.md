# Buildings

## Purpose
Owns the buildings of the game: the BuildType every other module names a building by, CD_Buildings - the one place a building is defined - the production menu that offers them, and placing them on the board: a ghost previewed on the free area nearest the centre (red at the centre when nothing fits), moved by pressing or dragging, placed with the green tick (cells occupied through the grid service) or dropped with the red cross.
