# Buildings

## Purpose
Owns the buildings of the game: the BuildType every other module names a building by, CD_Buildings - the one place a building is defined, filed in the Shared Scriptables of BuildingsSystemRoot's adapter and read by everyone (this module too) through `ISharedDataModel` - the production menu that offers them, and placing them on the board: a ghost previewed on the free area nearest the centre (red at the centre when nothing fits) with its name and footprint (`Barracks  4×4`) written under the confirm / cancel prompt, moved by pressing or dragging, placed with the green tick (cells occupied through the grid service) or dropped with the red cross. A placed building's data (type, area, hp) is kept by the grid alone, as the `BoardBuildingVO` its cells hold; this module keeps no record of it. Nothing about a selection is kept here either. The grid says what a press landed on: `Incoming.SelectBuilding(BoardBuildingVO)` announces `BuildingSelected(BuildingInfoVO)` - type, hp, and the door and spawn point turned into grid cells - and the info screen (GameplayModule's InfoScreen) shows it; `Incoming.ClearSelection` - a unit, a free cell, off the grid - announces `SelectionCleared`. While a building waits to be placed the grid picks nothing - `PlacementStarted` / `PlacementEnded` suspend and resume it - so a press only moves the ghost (`Incoming.PointerPressed`, heard for placement alone). Each unit card of the info screen carries its whole `UnitSpawnRequestVO`, so a click goes straight to the units module without coming back here. A building's `BoardBuildingVO.View` is its pooled `BoardBuilding` (under BuildingsSystemRoot) - no view maps data to objects; the commands in `Controllers/BoardBuildings/` work on it: `PlaceBoardBuildingCommand` (pool, occupy, fit sprite, lay the health bar), `ShowBoardBuildingHitCommand` (a struck building flashes and shows its health bar - `Incoming.BuildingDamaged`), `ReturnBoardBuildingCommand` (a destroyed one explodes and goes back to the pool - `Incoming.RemoveBuilding`). While an action runs (`RD_GameStatus`, read by `IPlacementModel.IsGameLocked`) no building is picked from the menu and the placement's tick and cross are not taken.

**Every value a designer tunes is in CD_Buildings**, not on a prefab or in code: per building its name, sprites, footprint, health, door and spawn point; for all of them the selection tint, the hit flash colour and duration, the health bar's width (share of the footprint) and inset (cells below the top edge), the explosion a destroyed building makes (sprites, number of puffs, puff width as a share of the footprint's longer side, stagger, duration), and the placement ghost's fits / blocked tints - `PreviewPlacementCommand` picks the tint and the view only applies it.

**Destroyed buildings explode.** `ReturnBoardBuildingCommand` takes `ExplosionCount` `BoardExplosion` puffs from the pool (group `board_buildings`, key `building_explosion` - pool keys are global, so Units has its own `unit_explosion`): the first at the footprint's centre, the rest at random points within 60 % of it, each `ExplosionStagger` after the one before; each grows from 40 % and fades over `ExplosionDuration` (DOTween scale + `DOTween.ToAlpha`), then goes back to the pool - the building's object goes back at once. Art: the same four Kenney Smoke Particles explosions (CC0) as Units, copied into `Art/Explosion/` (each module owns its sprites), in the Board atlas; sorting order 8. Static styling authored on a prefab (the prompt's buttons, the health bar's sprite colours) stays in the prefab.
