# Buildings

## Purpose
Owns the buildings of the game: the BuildType every other module names a building by, CD_Buildings - the one place a building is defined - the production menu that offers them, and placing them on the board: a ghost previewed on the free area nearest the centre (red at the centre when nothing fits), moved by pressing or dragging, placed with the green tick (cells occupied through the grid service) or dropped with the red cross. A placed building's data (type, area, hp) is kept by the grid alone, as the `BoardBuildingVO` its cells hold; this module keeps no record of it. Nothing about a selection is kept here either. The grid says what a press landed on: `Incoming.SelectBuilding(BoardBuildingVO)` announces `BuildingSelected(BuildingInfoVO)` - type, hp, and the door and spawn point turned into grid cells - and the info screen shows it; `Incoming.ClearSelection` - a unit, a free cell, off the grid - announces `SelectionCleared`. Neither acts while a building waits to be placed: then a press only moves the ghost (`Incoming.PointerPressed`, heard for placement alone). Each unit card of the info screen carries its whole `UnitSpawnRequestVO`, so a click goes straight to the units module without coming back here.
