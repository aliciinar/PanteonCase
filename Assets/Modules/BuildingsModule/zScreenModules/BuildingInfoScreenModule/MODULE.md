# BuildingInfoScreen

## Purpose
The selected building inside the information panel: its image, name and health, and for a building that produces units a card per unit. Each card is built with its whole `UnitSpawnRequestVO` - the unit, and the building's door and spawn cells from `BuildingInfoVO` - so clicking it announces `Outgoing.UnitRequested(request)`, which the Connector carries to the units module. No selection is remembered anywhere: the cards on screen are the selection.

## Concepts
building info, selection, information panel, unit production, UnitItem, UnitCardVO, UnitRequested, UnitSpawnRequestVO, object pooling
