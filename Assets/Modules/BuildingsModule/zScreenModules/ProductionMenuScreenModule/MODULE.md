# ProductionMenuScreen

## Purpose
The production menu on the left of the HUD: an endless, pooled scroll of the buildings the player can produce, in CD_Buildings' order, each card showing the building's `Icon` and name. CD_ProductionMenu lays out the grid (columns, card size, spacing).

Cards come from the pool only when the number of cards on screen changes (opening, a taller or shorter window, closing). While scrolling, a card whose row left the view stays in the content and is shown in a row that came in, so a scroll moves and rebinds cards and never reparents or deactivates one. The viewport has a canvas of its own, so the scrolling content re-batches without the rest of the HUD.
