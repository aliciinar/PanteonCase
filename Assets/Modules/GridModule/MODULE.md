# Grid

## Purpose
The board's System: the grid and everything standing on it. It is the one place a thing on the board keeps its data - every cell a building covers holds the same `BoardBuildingVO` (type, area, hp), a unit's cell its `BoardUnitVO` (type, hp, the cell it holds); both share `Area`, `MaxHp`, `Hp` and `View` - the object that shows it on the board, an `IOccupantView` (`Modules.Grid.Shared/Entities`, a marker: the grid knows no board object type) - on `CellOccupantVO`, so a cell leads to the object as it leads to the data. No other module keeps a copy, and only the grid writes them (`internal` setters, `InternalsVisibleTo("Modules.Grid")` in `Modules.Grid.Shared`). The data types live in `Modules.Grid.Shared`, and it references `Modules.Buildings.Shared` / `Modules.Units.Shared` for `BuildType` and `UnitType`.

It answers the board's questions through `IGridService` (bound cross-context, referenced from `Modules.Grid`) - which cell a world point is in, whether an area is free, how to keep an area on the grid, putting an occupant on an area (`Occupy`), moving a unit to another cell under the same instance (`MoveOccupant`), taking an occupant off the board (`Remove`), and the four searches: the free area nearest the centre (`FindNearestFreeAreaBfs`), the free cell nearest a cell (`FindNearestFreeCellBfs`), the free cell right next to an area nearest a given cell - where an attacker strikes from (`FindFreeCellAroundBfs`) and the shortest walk between two cells (`FindPathAStar`; what blocks a walk is the caller's `blockedBy`).

It decides what a press on the board landed on: `Incoming.PointerPressed` → `PickCellCommand` → exactly one of `Outgoing.BuildingPressed(BoardBuildingVO)`, `UnitPressed(BoardUnitVO)`, `EmptyPressed()` (a free cell or off the grid). A secondary press on the grid is an order: `Incoming.PointerSecondaryPressed` → `PickSecondaryCellCommand` → `Outgoing.FreeCellSecondaryPressed(cell)` (go there) or `OccupantSecondaryPressed(CellOccupantVO)` (attack it).

It keeps every occupant's health: `Incoming.DamageOccupant(occupant, damage)` → `DamageOccupantCommand` lowers `Hp` (never below 0) and announces `BuildingDamaged` / `UnitDamaged`, or at 0 takes it off the board (`Remove`) and announces `BuildingDestroyed` / `UnitDestroyed`. The module that owns the thing acts on it - Buildings selects a pressed building, Units a pressed unit. While a building is being placed (`Incoming.SuspendPicking` / `ResumePicking`, from Buildings' PlacementStarted / PlacementEnded) nothing is picked: a press only moves the building.

A search names its algorithm, and each is a Function in `Controllers/` that the service calls through the function provider. Lookups and geometry (`IsInside`, `WorldToCell`, `IsAreaFree`, ...) stay in the service: a search calls them once per cell and a drag once per frame, too often for the provider's pooling and boxing.
