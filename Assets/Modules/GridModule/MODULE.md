# Grid

## Purpose
The board's System: the grid and everything standing on it. It is the one place a thing on the board keeps its data - every cell a building covers holds the same `BoardBuildingVO` (type, area, hp), a unit's cell its `BoardUnitVO` (type, hp, the cell it holds and the `StepCell` it is walking through); no other module keeps a copy, and only the grid writes them (`internal` setters, `InternalsVisibleTo("Modules.Grid")` in `Modules.Grid.Shared`). The data types live in `Modules.Grid.Shared`, and it references `Modules.Buildings.Shared` / `Modules.Units.Shared` for `BuildType` and `UnitType`.

It answers the board's questions through `IGridService` (bound cross-context, referenced from `Modules.Grid`) - which cell a world point is in, whether an area is free, how to keep an area on the grid, putting an occupant on an area (`Occupy`), moving a unit to another cell under the same instance (`MoveOccupant`), where a walking unit is (`StepUnit`), and the three searches: the free area nearest the centre (`FindNearestFreeAreaBfs`), the free cell nearest a cell (`FindNearestFreeCellBfs`) and the shortest walk between two cells (`FindPathAStar`; what blocks a walk is the caller's `blockedBy`).

It decides what a press on the board landed on: `Incoming.PointerPressed` → `PickCellCommand` → exactly one of `Outgoing.BuildingPressed(BoardBuildingVO)`, `UnitPressed(BoardUnitVO)`, `EmptyPressed()` (a free cell or off the grid). A secondary press is answered only when it landed on a free cell of the grid: `Incoming.PointerSecondaryPressed` → `PickSecondaryCellCommand` → `Outgoing.FreeCellSecondaryPressed(cell)` - a move order. The module that owns the thing acts on it - Buildings selects a pressed building, Units a pressed unit. While a building is being placed (`Incoming.SuspendPicking` / `ResumePicking`, from Buildings' PlacementStarted / PlacementEnded) nothing is picked: a press only moves the building.

A search names its algorithm, and each is a Function in `Controllers/` that the service calls through the function provider. Lookups and geometry (`IsInside`, `WorldToCell`, `IsAreaFree`, ...) stay in the service: a search calls them once per cell and a drag once per frame, too often for the provider's pooling and boxing.
