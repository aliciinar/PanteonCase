# Grid

## Purpose
The board's grid as data: every cell and what occupies it, and the questions asked of them - which cell a world point is in, whether an area is free, how to keep an area on the grid, and the three searches: the free area nearest the centre (`FindNearestFreeAreaBfs`), the free cell nearest a cell (`FindNearestFreeCellBfs`) and the shortest walk between two cells (`FindPathAStar`). A Service: it knows no building or unit - what blocks a walk is the caller's `blockedBy` - and whoever uses the grid references `Modules.Grid` alone and calls `IGridService`.

A search names its algorithm, and each is a Function in `Controllers/` that the service calls through the function provider. Lookups and geometry (`IsInside`, `WorldToCell`, `IsAreaFree`, ...) stay in the service: a search calls them once per cell and a drag once per frame, too often for the provider's pooling and boxing.
